What changes

  1. First slot rotates with the calendar. The seasonal collection by default, swapped for the major holiday (Thanksgiving, Holiday, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day) during its selling window.
  2. New Occasions menu links all eight new occasion pages, plus Sympathy, which moves out of Flowers.
  3. New Locations menu puts every shop, design studio and Greater Boston delivery town in the main navigation, with Same-Day Delivery featured.
  4. Home and Office merge into one Home & Office menu, so the bar stays at nine items.
  5. Blog moves into Why Winston.
  6. Footer gets simpler: the 17 location links become four shops and two studios with addresses. Events, Home and Office merge into one Services column, and a Customer Care column is added.
