Flowers for Dyess Air Force Base Families

Military life means a lot of time apart, and flowers are one of the easiest ways to close the distance. We deliver birthday bouquets, anniversary roses, welcome home flowers and congratulations arrangements for promotions and retirements.

For deliveries on base, please include the recipient's full name, unit or building and a phone number we can reach them at. Base access rules can change, so give us a call at 325-672-7891 and we will tell you how we can get your flowers there, or deliver to their home in Abilene or Tye instead.

Dyess AFB Flower Delivery FAQs

Can you deliver flowers on Dyess Air Force Base?

Please call us at 325-672-7891 before ordering an on-base delivery. Base access rules change, and we will let you know the best way to get your flowers to your recipient.

What information do you need for a Dyess delivery?

The recipient's full name, unit or building, and a phone number. For a home off base, the street address is all we need.

Can I order from overseas or out of state?

Yes. Order online any time. Call us if you would like help choosing.

Do you have flowers for promotions and retirements?

Yes. We make arrangements, plants and centerpieces for ceremonies and celebrations. Call us and tell us about the event.

Is same-day delivery available?

Often, when you order early in the day. Call ahead for anything time-sensitive.
